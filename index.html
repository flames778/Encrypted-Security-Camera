Uncompromising Security

Built from the ground up to ensure your private spaces stay private.

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Military-grade encryption ensures both the confidentiality and authenticity of your footage. No one can read or tamper with it without your key.

Chunk-Based Storage

Video is continuously saved in encrypted, 10-second chunks, allowing for easy storage management and partial decryption when needed.

Smart Motion Detection

Powered by OpenCV, the camera intelligently detects movement and only records when necessary, saving massive amounts of disk space.

Raw H.264 Video

Utilizes ffmpeg to encode raw frames directly into efficient H.264 streams, maintaining high quality with zero latency before encryption.
